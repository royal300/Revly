## Slide 2: The Real Problem: Why Local Businesses Stay Invisible on Google
* **The Reality:** 88% of people looking for a cafe, salon, or doctor search on Google Maps before visiting.
* **The Problem:** Even if your shop has great service and dozens of daily customers, you don’t show up in the top search results.
* **Why?**
  * Competitors with more reviews and frequent customer posts take the top 3 spots ("Google Local 3-Pack").
  * When your business isn't in the top 3, over **75% of new potential customers never even know you exist**.

---

## Slide 3: The 3 Big Bottlenecks Every Business Owner Faces

### 1. Customers Leave Without Reviewing
* 95% of happy customers walk out the door without writing a review.
* Asking customers in person (*"Sir, please leave us a 5-star review"*) feels pushy and awkward for staff.
* Customers forget the moment they leave the premises.

### 2. The "Blank Page" Problem
* Even customers who want to support the business don't know what to write.
* Typing multiple paragraphs on a smartphone feels like homework.

### 3. The "Nice & Good" Trap (Zero Ranking Value)
* The few customers who do write reviews usually post 2 words: *"Nice food"*, *"Super salon"*, or *"Good service"*.
* **Why this fails:** Google's search engine looks for specific words. When someone searches for *"best cold coffee near me"* or *"hydra facial in Salt Lake"*, a review saying *"nice"* gives Google **zero reason to show your shop**.

### 4. Zero Customer Data
* Customers pay by cash or UPI and leave.
* The owner has no phone number, no visit history, and no way to invite them back.

## Slide 6: Core Business Features Built for Store Owners

### 1. Custom Feedback Questions by Industry
* Tailor questions to your exact business type:
  * **Cafes & Restaurants:** Food Taste, Service Speed, Ambience & Music, Cleanliness.
  * **Salons & Spas:** Stylist Skill, Hygiene, Waiting Time, Product Quality.
  * **Clinics & Healthcare:** Doctor Consultation, Care Quality, Waiting Time.
  * **Gyms & Fitness:** Equipment Quality, Trainer Guidance, Locker Cleanliness.
  * **Retail Shops:** Product Variety, Staff Helpfulness, Billing Speed.
* The system asks 3 quick questions so every customer review talks about different aspects of the business.

### 2. Customer Directory & Contact Storage (Mini-CRM)
* Collects customer name and mobile number voluntarily.
* Automatically tracks repeat visitors vs. first-time guests.
* Build a genuine, clean customer contact list for festival offers, updates, and loyalty rewards.

### 3. Business Health & Department Analytics
* Track total QR scans, reviews generated, and Google redirect rates.
* Department-level ratings (e.g. *Food: 4.9/5, Service: 4.1/5*).
* Catch internal operational problems (like slow service) before they become public complaints.

### 4. Custom Branded Tabletop Standers
* Customized with your shop's official colors and logo.
* High-resolution, print-ready QR standers for tables, billing counters, and waiting lounges.
